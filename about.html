About the ISAP Forum

An initiative of the University of Professional Studies, Accra, created to strengthen engagement between academia, industry, government and other stakeholders within the Information Systems field.

A platform where Information Systems meets real-world practice

The Information Systems Academics and Practitioners’ Forum, ISAP Forum, is an initiative of the University of Professional Studies, Accra, created to strengthen engagement between academia, industry, government and other stakeholders within the Information Systems field.

ISAP provides a platform for academics, practitioners, policymakers, researchers and students to exchange knowledge, examine emerging technology and policy issues, develop collaborative initiatives and connect Information Systems research with practical challenges.

Mission & Vision

What ISAP Stands For

Mission

To bridge the gap between theory and practice in Information Systems by providing a collaborative platform for knowledge exchange, research engagement, professional dialogue and policy discussion.

Vision

To become a leading interdisciplinary community influencing the development of Information Systems in Ghana and Africa through meaningful collaboration among academia, industry, government and society.

Core Objectives

What ISAP Sets Out to Achieve

Knowledge Exchange

Share academic research, industry experience, case studies and lessons from Information Systems practice.

Collaboration

Promote joint research, guest lectures, internships, curriculum engagement and collaborative publications.

Career Development

Connect students and early-career professionals with practitioners, mentors and organisations.

Critical Dialogue

Provide a platform for informed discussion of emerging technologies, cybersecurity, digital transformation, ethics and governance.

Research Translation

Make research useful to policymakers and practitioners while allowing real-world problems to inform academic research.
