01 · About the Forum

About ISAP Forum 2026

Ghana’s public identification ecosystem has developed from multiple sector-specific identification systems towards a more integrated digital identity environment.

ISAP Forum 2026 brings together government institutions, regulators, industry leaders, academics, researchers and students to examine Ghana’s experience with public sector identification systems, their contribution to socioeconomic development, current implementation challenges and the way forward.

The forum will provide a platform for dialogue on integration, interoperability, cybersecurity, data protection, institutional coordination, service delivery and the future of digital identity in Ghana.

02 · The Stakes

Why This Forum Matters

01

Digital Identity

Explore the evolution of public identification systems in Ghana and their growing role in national digital transformation.

02

Integration

Examine the relationship between sector-specific identification systems and efforts towards a more integrated national identity ecosystem.

03

Socioeconomic Development

Discuss how identification systems affect financial inclusion, healthcare, social protection, governance, elections and public service delivery.

04

Trust, Privacy & Security

Examine cybersecurity, data protection, privacy, inclusion, public trust and responsible management of identity data.
